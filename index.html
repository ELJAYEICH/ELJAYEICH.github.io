Auto Shorts Uploader

Auto Shorts Uploader is a personal project that helps its owner create short-form videos using AI-generated scripts, stock footage, narration, and captions.

How it works

The application generates video scripts, assembles video clips, creates narration and subtitles, and uploads the finished videos to the owner's YouTube channel after the owner authorizes access.

Privacy

YouTube authorization is used to upload videos to the connected channel. The application stores its credentials in protected GitHub Actions secrets and does not place them in its public website.
